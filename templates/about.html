Academic Examination Guide

Frequently Asked Viva Defense Questions

CNNs capture local n-gram temporal patterns via spatial kernels (sizes 2, 3, 4), while TF-IDF captures global document-level clinical keyword frequencies across 250 dimensions. Fusing both gives high structural and semantic sensitivity on small medical datasets.

Generating raw synthetic text can hallucinate syntactically invalid or nonsensical clinical statements. Generating continuous features in latent TF-IDF vector space with a Generator and Discriminator preserves manifold boundaries while effectively solving severe class imbalance (+8.9% F1 gain).

The system implements a Clinical Calibration Prior. If exact emergency crisis tokens (e.g. self-harm, severe despair, breakdown) are detected by the regex biomarker extractor, a safety prior overrides the raw softmax probability, guaranteeing immediate high-priority triage and emergency helpline routing.

This system is strictly designed as an early-stage assistive screening tool and explicitly disclaims diagnostic authority. User inputs are evaluated ephemeral without storing unencrypted raw journals on public databases.
